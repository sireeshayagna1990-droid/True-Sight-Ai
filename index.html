ARCHITECTURE & RESEARCH

How TrueSight AI Protects Truth

Combining spatial artifact localization, frequency domain spectral analysis, and multi-modal integrity cross-checks to counter generative manipulation.

01

Spatial & Frequency FFT Analysis

Generative diffusion models and GAN upsamplers leave telltale periodic grid artifacts in the frequency domain. TrueSight analyzes spatial pixel gradients and 2D Fourier transforms to spot non-organic high-frequency noise spikes.

02

Biometric & Lighting Invariance

Human eyes reflect light according to physical optics. We audit corneal specular reflections across both pupils to ensure light source angle consistency and check for blending seams along hair, neck, and ear boundaries.

03

Linguistic Perplexity & Stylometry

Large language models often exhibit repetitive burstiness and predictable token transitions. Our NLP analyzer models token entropy, deceptive urgency keywords, and formulaic clichés to flag synthetic texts.

04

Zero-Knowledge Local Privacy

Media privacy is paramount. TrueSight AI runs local file processing inside the user's browser runtime. Sensitive identity documents never leave the client's local session, ensuring ethical adherence to privacy standards.
